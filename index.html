Pujo Parikrama

Pandal hopping with your friends. Pick the name your group will know you by. After this, only the admin can change it.

What the admin can see
Your live location while this app is open, the photos you add, and your private messages. All of it is stored for safety and audit. Other friends cannot see any of it.
You can pause location sharing at any time with the button at the top of the app.

Admin sign in

Use the email and password you created in Supabase.
